For you, Chin

It was never about becoming the person everyone expected you to be. It was always about finding the life that feels like yours, a life you can genuinely call your own. You do not have to live according to someone else's timeline, expectations, or definition of success.

You do not have to rush becoming the person you want to be. You have your own path, your own pace, and so much ahead of you to discover.

I know there may be days when things feel uncertain, heavy, or difficult to understand. There may even be things from the past, thoughts, fears, or experiences that still bother or haunt you. But I genuinely hope that, little by little, you find the strength to face them and eventually overcome whatever has been weighing on your heart.

Please don't underestimate yourself. I believe there are so many things you are capable of conquering and pursuing. You don't have to have everything figured out right now. Just keep moving, keep learning, and keep giving yourself the chance to become who you want to be.

Keep discovering what makes you happy. Keep chasing the things that genuinely matter to you. Change your mind if you need to. Take a break when things become too much. Start again whenever you have to. None of those things mean you are failing. They simply mean you are growing and finding your way.

Your life does not have to look like anyone else's. You do not need to reach the same places, at the same time, or follow the same path just because everyone else seems to be moving in a certain direction. What matters is finding a life that feels like yours and becoming someone you are genuinely happy to be.

Whatever challenges come your way, I hope you remember that you are stronger and more capable than the doubts that sometimes try to hold you back. I hope you get to pursue the life, dreams, and happiness that you truly want, and that you find the courage to keep choosing yourself along the way.

I made this playlist hoping these songs can keep you company whenever you need them. Maybe on the days you need a little strength, a little peace, or simply a reminder that you can keep going.

And whatever it is that you are quietly carrying, I sincerely hope that one day, it no longer has power over you.

Take things one step at a time. You have more strength in you than you probably realize, Chin. Padayon!🌷

Keep going. We believe in you. With love and support
